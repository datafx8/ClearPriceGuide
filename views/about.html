About PriceDoctor

Empowering patients with transparent healthcare pricing to make informed decisions and save money

Our Mission

Healthcare shouldn't be a mystery. Yet millions of Americans face a broken system where prices are hidden, bills are confusing, and costs can vary by thousands of dollars for the same procedure—even within the same city.

PriceDoctor was created to change that.

We believe that everyone deserves access to transparent, easy-to-understand healthcare pricing information. Our mission is to empower patients to:

  • Compare prices across hospitals and providers in their area
  • Make informed decisions about where to receive care
  • Save thousands of dollars on medical procedures and services
  • Understand their options from virtual visits to emergency care
  • Navigate the healthcare system with confidence and clarity

We're building tools that cut through the confusion and put power back in the hands of patients. By aggregating price transparency data that hospitals are now required to publish, we make it simple to find quality care at fair prices.

Our vision: A healthcare system where transparent pricing is the norm, where patients can shop for care as easily as any other service, and where quality and affordability go hand in hand.

Join us in making healthcare pricing transparent, accessible, and fair for everyone.
